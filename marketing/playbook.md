# The playbook

Producer: read before every script. Style only: your accuracy and safety rules and limits always win. All of it is hypothesis.

## Two narrators

Odd numbers: **the Raconteur.** Relishes words, understates, mock-solemn about absurd things, one aside per script. Even: **the Reckoner.** Quick and playful; makes numbers physical ("five times the book he learned it from"), says how we know, says ranges aloud. Original characters, imitating no one. Each talks to one listener.

## Standing rules

1. **Open mid-scene on the strangest true moment, with a person in it.** Hold back the cause: the cause is the idea.
2. **Line two hints where we're going.**
3. **Let the viewer guess wrong:** set up an assumption the story reports, then overturn it.
4. **The idea is the payoff,** named once, the moment it explains the opening.
5. **After the payoff, one line at most, then the sign-off.** No moral, no "it matters because".
6. **Write for the ear.** Sentences under 14 words. Humour from understatement, never about real suffering.
7. **Pictures:** open on one figure doing something odd. Change at each beat; hold one engraving through the reveal.
8. **Caption:** a different angle from the hook, naming the idea in words a stranger would search. Then "A true story, fact-checked." Then "Tomorrow: <the next number's topic, from the guide's rotation>." End "Curiouser No. N".
9. **Hashtags:** #curiouser, the topic, the idea, the subject, #todayilearned. Never #fyp or #viral.

## This experiment: how should it end?

Only the line before the sign-off changes. Each narrator gets both arms.

- **The button** (Nos. 13, 14, 17, 18, 21, 22…): one more true detail from the story that recolours it.
- **The ritual** (Nos. 12, 15, 16, 19, 20, 23…): "Today's word: …" (Raconteur) or "Today's number: …" (Reckoner), from the story.
- **Measure:** shares plus follows per 1,000 views, at two days or older, on the platform Carl reports most.
- **It counts if** one arm's median is at least half as much again and leads in both halves of the run. Judge after six reported posts per arm.
