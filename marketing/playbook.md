# The playbook

Producer: read before every script. Style only; your accuracy, safety and limits win.

## Two narrators, chosen by the story

Choose by story; add `"narrator": "raconteur"` or `"reckoner"` to the script. Never bend a story to fit. Ties go to whoever has fewer of the last seven.

- **The Raconteur**, for payoffs about people, words or irony. Wry, understated, mock-solemn about absurd things; one aside at most.
- **The Reckoner**, for payoffs about numbers, measurement, scale or how we know. Quick and playful; makes the story's numbers physical, says ranges aloud.

Both original; imitate no one.

## Standing rules

1. **Open mid-scene on the strangest true moment, with a person in it.** Hold back the cause: the cause is the idea.
2. **Line two hints where we're going.**
3. **Let the viewer guess wrong:** set up an assumption the story reports, then overturn it.
4. **The idea is the payoff,** named once, the moment it explains the opening.
5. **After the payoff, one line at most, then the sign-off.** No moral.
6. **Write for the ear.** Sentences under 14 words. Never joke about real suffering.
7. **Pictures:** open on one figure doing something odd; hold one engraving through the reveal.
8. **Caption:** a different angle from the hook, naming the idea in words a stranger would search. Then "A true story, fact-checked." Then "Tomorrow: <next topic in the rotation>." End "Curiouser No. N".
9. **Hashtags:** #curiouser, the topic, the idea, the subject, #todayilearned. Never #fyp or #viral.

## This experiment: how should it end?

Only the line before the sign-off changes.

- **The button** (odd numbers, from No. 13): one more true detail from the story that recolours it.
- **The ritual** (even numbers, from No. 12): "Today's word: …" (Raconteur) or "Today's number: …" (Reckoner), from the story.
- **Measure:** shares plus follows per 1,000 views, at two days or older, on the platform Carl reports most.
- **It counts if** one arm's median is at least half as much again and leads in both halves of the run. Judge after six reported posts per arm.
