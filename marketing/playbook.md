# The playbook

Producer: read this before every script. It shapes style only: your accuracy and safety rules and fixed limits always win. Everything here is a hypothesis until Carl's numbers say otherwise.

## Standing rules (hypotheses)

1. **One story, one turn, one idea.** Cut any name, date or number that doesn't carry the turn.
2. **Trouble by word 40.** By then the viewer knows what went wrong or what doesn't add up.
3. **Name the idea late:** after the turn, in the last third, once.
4. **Write for the ear.** Most sentences under 14 words, one thought each. No brackets, no lists.
5. **Last line before the sign-off:** where the viewer would meet this idea today. No moral, no instruction.
6. **Tone:** dry, exact, amused. Never "you won't believe", "did you know", or a question the video doesn't answer.
7. **Pictures:** open on the engraving a stranger would stop for (one figure doing something odd), not the most literal match. Change picture at each beat. Never reuse yesterday's opener.
8. **Caption:** the first sentence tells the story and names the idea, in words a stranger would search for. Then "A true story, fact-checked." (only after a pass or fixed verdict). End "Curiouser No. N". The AI-voice label is added for you; don't write it.
9. **Hashtags:** #curiouser, the topic (#history), the idea (#goodhartslaw), the subject (#hanoi), #todayilearned. Never #fyp or #viral.

## This experiment: which opening stops the scroll?

Only the form of the hook changes. It is still ten words at most and true to the checked story.

- **Odd-numbered stories, the scene:** when, where, what happened. "In 1902, Hanoi paid a bounty for every rat's tail."
- **Even-numbered stories, the contradiction:** the result that shouldn't have happened, cause held back. "Hanoi paid for dead rats and got rat farms."
- **Measure:** views at two days, on the platform Carl reports most.
- **It counts if** one form's median is at least half as much again as the other's and it leads in both halves of the run.
- **Judge after** six reported posts of each form. Until then, nothing else here changes.
