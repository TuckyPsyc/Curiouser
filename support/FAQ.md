# Questions readers ask

Answers that are known to be true, for drafting replies. If a question isn't covered here or in `README.md` and `DECISIONS.md`, don't guess: flag it for Carl.

## Installing and updating

**How do I install it?**
Android only, for now.
1. On your phone, open https://github.com/TuckyPsyc/curiouser/releases/latest in Chrome.
2. Tap **curiouser.apk** to download it.
3. Open the **Files** app, tap **Downloads**, then tap **curiouser.apk** and **Install**.
4. If asked, allow installing unknown apps.

**"There was a problem parsing the package."**
That happens when the download is opened from Chrome's pop-up. Open it from **Files → Downloads** instead. This is what worked on Carl's own phone (a Nokia on Android 14).

**"App not installed."**
Restart the phone and try again from **Files → Downloads**.

**How do I get new features?**
Open the app, close it, and open it again. Updates download the first time and appear the second. No reinstall is needed. Only a new version of the app itself needs installing again, from the same link, and Carl will say when that happens.

**Do I need to update to get new stories?**
No. Stories arrive by themselves each morning.

**Is there an iPhone version?**
Not yet. A web version for iPhone is planned.

## Using it

**When does the story arrive?**
By 7:00 each morning, UK time, with a reminder at 7:00. To switch the reminder off, open **The Shelves**, scroll to the bottom and turn off **The White Rabbit**.

**There's no new story today.**
Every story is checked before it goes out. If the check finds a problem, that day's story is held back and the previous one stays. It is deliberate: we'd rather be a day late than wrong.

**What counts towards the Hatter's watch?**
A day counts when you reach the end of any story or use Drink Me. Once a week (Monday to Sunday) a Hatter's Hour stops the clock on one missed day. Weeks in a row needs just one story a week, and the total of days read never goes down.

**What is the Caucus-race?**
Five questions each Sunday on the week's stories, the same for everyone. It stays open until the next Sunday, and everyone who finishes gets a comfit.

**What are Drink Me and Eat Me?**
Drink Me shrinks the story to its one-line idea. Eat Me grows it back, and from the full story you can go down the rabbit hole for a deeper dive.

## Privacy and data

**What do you know about me?**
Nothing. There are no accounts and no tracking. Your streak, jars and quiz results are stored only on your phone and never sent anywhere. The only thing the app fetches is the public story files.

**I got a new phone / reinstalled, and my streak and jars have gone.**
They live only on the phone, so deleting the app, clearing its data or changing phone loses them. They can't be restored, because we never had a copy.

**What happens to my email?**
It sits in Curiouser's mailbox so Carl can reply. An AI assistant reads it to draft a reply, which Carl checks and sends himself. It isn't added to any list or shared with anyone else. Ask and it will be deleted.

## The stories

**Who writes them?**
An AI (Claude) researches and writes each story, a second AI checks every name, date, number, quotation and link independently before anyone sees it, and Carl is the editor-in-chief. Every story lists its sources at the bottom.

**I think a story is wrong.**
Thank you: please say which story and what looks wrong. It will be checked again, and corrections reach your phone automatically.

**Can I suggest a topic or a feature?**
Yes. Suggestions go to Carl.

**Does it cost anything?**
No.
